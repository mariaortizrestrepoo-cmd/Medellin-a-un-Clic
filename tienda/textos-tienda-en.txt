NAME
Medellín a un clic – Guide

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Your Medellín guide in English and Spanish: places, food, transport and SOS help

FULL DESCRIPTION
Medellín a un clic is your travel guide to enjoy Medellín and Antioquia with peace of mind. It's in English and Spanish, by Mokea Digital LLC.

Important: this app is for travelers who already have access to Medellín a un clic. Sign in with the email linked to your access, and you'll receive a code to unlock it.

WHAT YOU'LL FIND
• Must-see places: Comuna 13, Plaza Botero, viewpoints, Pueblito Paisa, Parque Arví and more.
• Day trips: Guatapé and El Peñol, Río Claro, Hacienda Nápoles, Jardín, Jericó, Santa Fe de Antioquia and the Eastern Route.
• Local food: what to try and where to eat it, from bandeja paisa to coffee.
• Nightlife and where to stay.
• Getting around: Metro, Metrocable, taxis, the airport and tips to move around safely.
• Festivals and traditions: the Flower Festival, Christmas lights and paisa culture.

MADE FOR TRAVELERS
• Emergencies (SOS): call 123 with one tap, find the U.S. Embassy phone number and send your location on WhatsApp to someone you trust.
• Near me: see which places are closest to you. Your location stays on your phone; we don't save it.
• Useful phrases with audio: tap a phrase and your phone says it out loud in Spanish, with its pronunciation and translation.
• Favorites: save the places you want to visit.
• Search to find any place fast.
• Works without internet: tap the 'Use it without internet' button and the app saves everything on your phone.

TOURS WITH PAOLA
Paola Tobón, your trusted person in Medellín, takes you on day trips with transportation. Message her on WhatsApp from the app to book and ask about prices. Tours are a separate service and are not included in the app.

Some images are illustrative, created with artificial intelligence. Prices and opening hours of places may change: confirm them before you go.
